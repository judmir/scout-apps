How it works

  1. Three team-app icons (TeamSnap, GameChanger, Heja) with a "Copy calendar link" tap.

  3. One week view fills up; a red "overlap Sat 10:00" flag shows.
  4. A phone lock screen gets a push: "Leo's game moved to 11:30, Field 4."
