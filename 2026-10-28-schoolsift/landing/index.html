How it works

  1. A phone buzzes with a pile of ParentSquare/ClassDojo/Remind notices (spirit week ×4, board meeting, bus update, permission slip).
  2. They slide into SchoolSift; duplicates stack and merge, district posts fade into "Muted: 37".
  3. Two cards remain: "Mia · permission slip + $12 · due Fri" and "Leo · early release Wed 1:30".
  4. 7 pm: one quiet notification, "2 things need you tomorrow." Phone goes face-down.

Preview notes (validation test, not shown to visitors)

Ad angle: an 8-second Meta video. A phone lights up at 5:02 am: "It's spirit week next week, support your favorite team!" (12th time). Cut to SchoolSift at 7 pm: "2 things need you tomorrow." Hook: "Kids at 2 schools? Get only the school posts that are for your kid." Target US parents 28–50 with school-age children (Meta ads).

Test: - Validation page: the two-button page (below) with "Start for $3.99/month" and a softer waitlist link. - Ads: €30–50 of Meta ads to US parents of school-age children (interests: PTA, ParentSquare/ClassDojo-style school apps, parenting). - Bar: 100+ US visitors; >3% price clicks is strong, <1% is a kill. - Accuracy check before building: take 30 real (anonymised) ParentSquare/ClassDojo emails and screenshots, run them through a small LLM with the triage prompt and count how many land on the right kid with the right "needs you / muted" call. One evening's work.
